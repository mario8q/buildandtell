How Build&Tell works

Three steps between an idea and a story worth reading.

  1. 01

    Start a project

    Give it a title, a description and a cover. Set a status — planning, in progress, completed or archived — so the reader always knows where things stand.

  2. 02

    Log as you go

    Capture features, fixes, bugs, experiments and milestones the moment they happen. Each entry is typed and time-stamped, so the trail stays honest.

  3. 03

    Share the story

    Every project gets a public page linked from your builder profile. Add your website and GitHub, and let people read how the thing actually came to be.
